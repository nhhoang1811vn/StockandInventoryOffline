# StockandInventory

Local/guest-first stock and inventory listing. Visual language follows the Online version; this app
remains a separate product.

## Language

**Store**:
A local inventory workspace.
_Avoid_: workspace, shop, warehouse

**Good**:
A stockable item.
_Avoid_: product, item

**Group**:
A folder of goods.

**Document**:
An incoming or outgoing stock movement.
_Avoid_: order, invoice, receipt

**Wallet**:
A money account used to record payments.
_Avoid_: bank, account, cash register

**Payment**:
Money inbound or outbound against a wallet.
_Avoid_: transaction

**Online version**:
Stock Inventory Online — visual and information-architecture reference only, not this app’s runtime.
_Avoid_: cloud app, v2, collaboration app (when meaning the UI source)

**This app / StockandInventory**:
The local/guest-first listing.
_Avoid_: offline app (in user-facing copy unless contrasting with the Online version)
